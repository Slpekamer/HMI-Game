# Rejected common-melee crystal-fan presentation

This completed visual experiment uses the older rule-2 instantaneous-ray gameplay baseline. It is not the newer real-projectile integrated game and must not be substituted for it.

The proposed shared-melee cue was a short pale-lilac crystal fan, distinct from cyan ranged ribbons and warm hurt feedback. It appeared on genuine Melee events and faded without introducing charge-up, changing damage or enlarging attack range. Its direction represented the recorded event direction, not a measured weapon trajectory. A fixed display layer and camera-related plane were presentation approximations. Very short or invalid directions could be suppressed rather than inventing a false sweep.

The owner was bounded to one active cue and at most two onsets per second, with active-period suppression, a dark-render opportunity and lifecycle identity checks. Those are presentation budgets, not guarantees that every hit is shown. Normal depth and existing target hurt coloration were preserved.

Semantic tests and natural-rule controlled route comparisons passed within their stated fields, but the first native full-scale onset failed visible-shape acceptance. The new cue did not form a clear continuous fan or attack direction beside the actor and the simultaneous cyan ranged effect. The first pair was stopped rather than repeating five more angle cases to find a favorable picture. Expiry restored the world region to the baseline. Source/first-draw consistency was not treated as proof of readable art.

Later investigation showed that hiding the ranged draw affected the overlapping image region, but did not prove that every purple fragment belonged to the fan or isolate a complete rendering cause. That diagnostic is not a product proposal to hide ranged attacks. The experiment remains rejected and unintegrated; no geometry/anchor scan is continued by this archive.

Recorded semantic coverage included 880 owner checks plus 11 actual-Host checks; the native first pair used 177 updates and exited normally. Strict editor/import diagnostics and the incomplete visual-angle coverage remain explicit. This is not all-five-hero, compact-short-distance, device or crowded-combat acceptance.
