# Candy arena presentation

`arena_icing.svg` is original code-authored vector art for a quiet, grid-free poured-icing floor. It contains no generated screenshots, UI or actors and uses no third-party textures.

Rounded boundary bonbons, wafer reveals and cake houses are procedural geometry in `CandyArenaVisuals.cs`. Existing food-hero, enemy, wall-block and menu assets and their provenance are unchanged. The final grid-free comparison passed owner visual review. This remains an independent candidate, not a merge or device certification.
